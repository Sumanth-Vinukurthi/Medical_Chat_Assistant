Paracetamol
- Use: Fever, Headache, Pain relief
- Dosage: 500 mg to 1 gram every 4-6 hours (max 4 grams/day)
- Side Effects: Nausea, allergic reactions, liver damage (in high doses)
- Precautions: Avoid alcohol, liver disease patients should use with caution.

Ibuprofen
- Use: Pain relief, Inflammation, Fever
- Dosage: 200 mg to 400 mg every 4-6 hours (max 1.2 grams/day)
- Side Effects: Stomach upset, ulcers, kidney issues
- Precautions: Avoid in pregnant women, especially in the third trimester, caution 
in patients with kidney disease.

Aspirin
- Use: Pain relief, Anti-inflammatory, Blood thinner (for heart conditions)
- Dosage: 325 mg every 4-6 hours (max 4 grams/day)
- Side Effects: Stomach irritation, bleeding risks, allergic reactions
- Precautions: Avoid in children with viral infections (due to risk of Reye's 
syndrome), avoid in patients with bleeding disorders.

Diclofenac
- Use: Pain relief, Inflammation (Arthritis, injuries)
- Dosage: 50 mg to 75 mg every 8-12 hours
- Side Effects: Stomach pain, dizziness, liver issues
- Precautions: Use cautiously in elderly, avoid in patients with peptic ulcers.

Amoxicillin
- Use: Bacterial infections (antibiotic)
- Dosage: 250 mg to 500 mg every 8 hours (for adults)
- Side Effects: Nausea, diarrhea, allergic reactions
- Precautions: Allergies to penicillin, use with caution in people with kidney 
problems.

Hyoscine
- Use: Motion sickness, Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS)
- Dosage: 0.3 mg to 0.6 mg every 8 hours
- Side Effects: Drowsiness, blurred vision, dry mouth
- Precautions: Avoid in glaucoma, caution in elderly with prostate problems.

Cetirizine
- Use: Allergy symptoms (Hay fever, rash, hives)
- Dosage: 10 mg once daily
- Side Effects: Drowsiness, dry mouth
- Precautions: Caution in people with liver disease or epilepsy.

Metformin
- Use: Type 2 Diabetes (Blood sugar control)
- Dosage: 500 mg to 1000 mg twice daily
- Side Effects: Nausea, abdominal discomfort, lactic acidosis (rare)
- Precautions: Avoid in patients with kidney issues or metabolic acidosis.

Losartan
- Use: Hypertension, Heart failure
- Dosage: 50 mg once daily
- Side Effects: Dizziness, fatigue, high potassium levels
- Precautions: Avoid in pregnancy, monitor kidney function.

Azithromycin
- Use: Bacterial infections (respiratory, skin, and ear infections)
- Dosage: 500 mg on day 1, followed by 250 mg for the next 4 days
- Side Effects: Nausea, diarrhea, stomach pain
- Precautions: Use cautiously in patients with liver problems or QT prolongation.

Ciprofloxacin
- Use: Bacterial infections (urinary tract, respiratory tract, skin)
- Dosage: 250 mg to 500 mg every 12 hours
- Side Effects: Nausea, diarrhea, tendonitis, dizziness
- Precautions: Avoid in pregnant women and children, caution in patients with tendon
problems.

Prednisolone
- Use: Inflammation, autoimmune disorders, allergies
- Dosage: 5 mg to 10 mg per day (adjust as needed)
- Side Effects: Weight gain, increased blood sugar, high blood pressure, mood swings
- Precautions: Use cautiously in people with diabetes, peptic ulcers, or 
hypertension.

Clindamycin
- Use: Bacterial infections (skin, bone, respiratory)
- Dosage: 150 mg to 450 mg every 6-8 hours
- Side Effects: Nausea, diarrhea, abdominal cramps, rash
- Precautions: Use cautiously in people with a history of gastrointestinal disease, 
particularly colitis.

Fexofenadine
- Use: Allergy symptoms (hay fever, hives)
- Dosage: 60 mg twice a day or 180 mg once a day
- Side Effects: Drowsiness (rare), headache, dry mouth
- Precautions: Use cautiously in elderly patients, avoid alcohol.

Lisinopril
- Use: Hypertension, heart failure
- Dosage: 10 mg to 40 mg once daily
- Side Effects: Dizziness, headache, hyperkalemia
- Precautions: Avoid during pregnancy, monitor kidney function.

Metoprolol
- Use: Hypertension, heart failure, angina
- Dosage: 25 mg to 100 mg once daily
- Side Effects: Fatigue, dizziness, depression, slow heart rate
- Precautions: Caution in people with asthma or bronchospasm, avoid abrupt 
withdrawal.

Gabapentin
- Use: Nerve pain, seizures, restless legs syndrome
- Dosage: 300 mg to 600 mg three times a day
- Side Effects: Dizziness, fatigue, weight gain, blurred vision
- Precautions: Use cautiously in elderly patients, avoid alcohol.

Fluconazole
- Use: Fungal infections (yeast infections, cryptococcal meningitis)
- Dosage: 150 mg as a single dose for vaginal yeast infection, or 200 mg daily for 
systemic infections
- Side Effects: Nausea, headache, liver toxicity (rare)
- Precautions: Use cautiously in people with liver disease, pregnant women should 
avoid.

Amlodipine
- Use: Hypertension, angina (chest pain)
- Dosage: 5 mg to 10 mg once daily
- Side Effects: Swelling of the ankles, dizziness, headache
- Precautions: Use with caution in patients with heart failure, monitor for 
peripheral edema.

Loratadine
- Use: Allergy symptoms (hay fever, hives)
- Dosage: 10 mg once daily
- Side Effects: Headache, dry mouth, drowsiness (rare)
- Precautions: Avoid alcohol and sedating agents.

Tamsulosin
- Use: Benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH), urinary retention
- Dosage: 0.4 mg once daily
- Side Effects: Dizziness, headache, abnormal ejaculation
- Precautions: Caution in patients with hypotension, use with caution in patients 
with liver disease.

Lorazepam
- Use: Anxiety, insomnia, seizure disorders
- Dosage: 2 mg to 4 mg per day, divided into 2-3 doses
- Side Effects: Drowsiness, dizziness, confusion, memory impairment
- Precautions: Avoid alcohol, use cautiously in elderly or debilitated patients.

Ranitidine
- Use: Acid reflux, ulcers, heartburn
- Dosage: 150 mg to 300 mg daily, depending on the condition
- Side Effects: Headache, dizziness, constipation
- Precautions: Avoid in patients with kidney problems, may interfere with certain 
lab tests.

Metronidazole
- Use: Bacterial and parasitic infections (intestinal, vaginal, skin)
- Dosage: 250 mg to 500 mg every 8 hours
- Side Effects: Nausea, metallic taste, dizziness
- Precautions: Avoid alcohol during treatment, may cause neurological side effects 
in long-term use.

Simvastatin
- Use: Cholesterol reduction (hyperlipidemia)
- Dosage: 10 mg to 40 mg daily (typically at bedtime)
- Side Effects: Muscle pain, liver enzyme abnormalities, digestive issues
- Precautions: Monitor liver function, avoid grapefruit juice.

Omeprazole
- Use: GERD, stomach ulcers
- Dosage: 20 mg to 40 mg daily
- Side Effects: Headache, nausea, diarrhea
- Precautions: Prolonged use can lead to vitamin B12 deficiency, bone fractures.

Furosemide
- Use: Diuretic (Edema, Hypertension)
- Dosage: 20 mg to 40 mg daily (can be adjusted)
- Side Effects: Dehydration, electrolyte imbalances, dizziness
- Precautions: Monitor kidney function, avoid in patients with low potassium.

Doxycycline
- Use: Bacterial infections (pneumonia, acne, Lyme disease)
- Dosage: 100 mg to 200 mg daily
- Side Effects: Nausea, photosensitivity (increased sun sensitivity)
- Precautions: Avoid in pregnant women and children under 8 years old, take with 
plenty of water to avoid esophageal irritation.

Fentanyl
- Use: Severe pain relief (for cancer, post-surgery)
- Dosage: 12 mcg/hr to 100 mcg/hr transdermal patch (depending on patient’s 
condition)
- Side Effects: Drowsiness, respiratory depression, constipation
- Precautions: Avoid in opioid-naive patients, monitor for overdose signs, avoid 
alcohol.

Sertraline
- Use: Depression, anxiety, OCD, PTSD
- Dosage: 25 mg to 200 mg daily
- Side Effects: Nausea, insomnia, sexual dysfunction, headache
- Precautions: Use cautiously in patients with a history of seizures or serotonin 
syndrome.

Hydrochlorothiazide
- Use: Diuretic (Hypertension, Edema)
- Dosage: 12.5 mg to 50 mg daily
- Side Effects: Low potassium, dizziness, dehydration, increased blood sugar
- Precautions: Use cautiously in patients with diabetes or gout, monitor 
electrolytes.

Baclofen
- Use: Muscle spasm (due to multiple sclerosis, spinal cord injury)
- Dosage: 5 mg to 20 mg three times daily
- Side Effects: Drowsiness, dizziness, weakness, headache
- Precautions: Avoid alcohol, monitor for CNS depression, use cautiously in patients
with renal impairment.

Fluticasone
- Use: Allergies, asthma (nasal spray, inhaler)
- Dosage: 1-2 sprays in each nostril once daily for nasal use, 44 mcg to 110 mcg 
inhaled twice daily for asthma
- Side Effects: Nasal irritation, headache, throat irritation, hoarseness
- Precautions: Use cautiously in patients with glaucoma or cataracts, avoid use in 
people with recent nasal surgery.
